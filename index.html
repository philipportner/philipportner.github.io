Philipp Ortner

I am a PhD student at the DAMS Lab (Data Management for Data Science laboratory) at the Technical University of Berlin
under the supervision of Matthias Boehm and Patrick Damme. I am involved in the DAPHNE and FONDA projects.

My research interests lie in the systems programming domain,
more specifically, compilers, database systems, and machine learning systems.

I did my master's on code generation in an MLIR-based compiler for Large-Scale Data Management, HPC, and Machine Learning Systems.
Before that, I wrote my undergraduate thesis in the Secure Systems group led by Daniel Gruss at the IAIK, where I implemented new versions of the Spectre attack.
